01 / Who we are

Local action.
Lasting possibility.

The Foundation for Health, Environment and Poverty Alleviation is a community-focused organisation committed to helping people develop the skills and confidence to improve their livelihoods. Our work connects health, environmental responsibility and practical training — including technology — with a shared goal: alleviating poverty.

03 / Our ICT initiative

BAVUBUKA

Digital skills. Real opportunity.

BAVUBUKA is our ICT skilling initiative, created to help individuals build practical technology skills for learning, work, entrepreneurship and greater independence. It is one way we turn our poverty alleviation mission into action.
